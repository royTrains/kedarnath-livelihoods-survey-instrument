# Enumerator script

Read each question as written. Notes in italics are for you, not for the respondent. Each chapter has a short Hindi paragraph under the English opener, for reference.

## Chapter 2: You and your household

*Let's start with you and the people you live with. Take your time with the counts; a rough number is fine.*

> आइए आपके और आपके घर के लोगों से शुरू करें। गिनती में अंदाज़ा ठीक है।

- How old are you? (completed years)
- Record sex; ask if unsure: are you male or female?
- Who is the head of your household — is it you, or someone else? If someone else, how are they related to you?
- What is your native language, the language you first learned at home?
- Which language is it? Write down exactly what they say.
- Are you currently married, never married, or widowed/divorced/separated?
- Do you know exactly how many years of schooling you completed, counting from class 1?
  - *Note for you:* A level is NOT a No. If they answer '8th', '12th pass', 'ITI', 'BA', 'MA' — code YES and convert it on the next screen. Code No only if they cannot say how far they went at all.
- How many years of schooling did you complete in total? (0 if none)
  - *Note for you:* Enter a NUMBER counted from class 1, never a level. Class 1 to 12 = that class number. ITI or diploma after 10th = 12. Bachelors = 15, or 16 if the course ran four years — ASK how long it ran. Masters = 17, or 18 after a four-year bachelors. If they dropped out part way through a year, count the completed years only.
- Which is closest: no formal education; some schooling but did not finish primary; finished primary but not secondary; or finished secondary or higher?
- How many members live in your household, including you?
- Counting everyone in your household aged 10 or above, has at least one of them finished six years of schooling or more?
  - *Note for you:* The respondent is himself a household member. If HE has six years or more the answer is Yes — do not send him looking for someone else. The screen is skipped automatically wherever his own answer already settles it.
- Is your family nuclear — just you, your spouse and unmarried children — or joint, living with parents, married siblings or other extended family? (Say single-member if you live alone.)
- How many of them, including you, earn money?
- Among those who earn money, are you the main income earner in your household?
- How many of them are children under 6 years old?
- And how many are aged 6 to 14?
- How many of these children do not go to school now?

## Chapter 3: Home, and the year away from it

*Now I'd like to know where your home is, and how your year moves between here and there.*

> अब बताइए आपका घर कहाँ है, और साल का कितना समय आप वहाँ और यहाँ रहते हैं।

- Where is your permanent home?
- Which state is your permanent home in?
- Is that home in a village, or in a town or city?
- When the Yatra closes for the season, do YOU go to your home place?
- For most of the year, do the rest of your household live in your home village or town?
- How many years have you been coming here for the Yatra season?
- What was the main reason you first came here to work?
- What was that reason? Write down what they say.
- Last year, in the months when the Yatra was closed, did you work away from your home place?
- Where did you go, and what work did you do there? Write what they say.
- In the past year, which month did you last leave here and go to your home place?
- And which month did you come back here?
- If this work here ended, would you go away from your home place to look for work in the coming year?
- Who mainly helped you get this work, or arranged it for you?
- Who was it? Write what they say.

## Chapter 4: Your work

*Tell me about the work you do here, in your own words. First, what you were doing last month.*

> अब यहाँ के काम के बारे में बताइए, अपने शब्दों में। पहले बताइए पिछले महीने आप क्या कर रहे थे।

- During the last month, what were you mainly doing? Ask it open and code the closest one; do not read the list out.
  - *Note for you:* Code what they were MAINLY doing, by time, over the last month. Work for pay = full-time if it was their main activity on most working days, part-time or occasional if it was picked up here and there around something else. Seasonal preparation (buying stock, repairing tack, bringing animals up) IS working for pay — it is their trade — so code 1 or 2, not 10. Unemployed (8) means looking for work and not finding it; between seasons with work expected is not unemployed. Code 10 is the genuine none: not working, not studying, not looking.
- What is your main work during the Yatra season? Ask what they do and code the closest group; do not read the list aloud unless needed.
- In your own words, what exactly is your main work here? Write down what they say — what they actually do, and who for. Do not tick a box for this one.
- In this main work, are you: working on your own account without hired workers; running a business with hired workers; a regular monthly wage-earner; or a daily/casual wage-earner?
- Who employs you? A private company; a government or public body; a household (domestic work); a contractor or agent; or an individual employer?
  - *Note for you:* ENUMERATOR: private company = a registered firm or hotel chain; government or public body = a government department, Yatra board, PWD or other public office; household = a family that hires them for domestic work; contractor or agent = a thekedar or agent who arranged and pays for the work; individual employer = one person who pays them directly, such as a shop or dhaba owner. If the respondent says 'the owner' or 'the boss' without more, ask who arranged the job. If they came through an agent (see the migration referral question), code 4. If unsure, code 97 and write the words in the notes.
- Besides your main work, how many OTHER paid activities or income-generating activities do you have? (0 if none. For example, a shop owner who also rents out a pony and drives in the off-season would say 2.)
- Which ones are they? Check every one that applies. Ask and code the closest group for each; do not read the list aloud unless needed.
  - *Note for you:* ENUMERATOR: hauling luggage or goods on the back, shoulders or a head-load, for pilgrims or shops, is code 3 (porter). Carrying a person in a kandi is also code 3; carrying a person on a palanquin as part of a team is code 4. Hauling luggage or goods on a pony or mule is code 1 if the respondent works the animal for someone else, and code 2 if they own it.
- Taking all of that other work together, about how much do you usually earn from it in a month? (Rs)
- Have you been in this job for less than one year?
  - *Note for you:* ENUMERATOR: tick Yes if the person has been in this job for less than one year. Do not ask for months. If Yes, skip the years question.
- If one year or more: how many years have you been in this job? Write it as a decimal if needed, for example 1.5 for one year and six months.
  - *Note for you:* ENUMERATOR: only if the person has been in this job one year or more. Write the number of years; use a decimal for part years (1.5 = one year and six months).
- In the Yatra season, on a normal working day, how many hours do you work in total, across all your work?
- How many days a week do you usually work in the season?
- In the last ten years, did you change your main kind of work?
- What was your previous main work? Write down exactly what they say, in their words — do not pick from a list.
- If you could not continue this work at all, what work would you move to instead? Write down exactly what they say, in their words — do not pick from a list, and do not prompt with examples.
- What was the main reason you changed?
- Apart from school and college, have you ever completed any training course or apprenticeship?

> अब हम बारह महीने एक-एक करके देखेंगे। कुछ महीने आप यहाँ काम कर रहे थे, कुछ घर पर। अंदाज़ा भी चलेगा।

- In May, what was your main work or activity?
- In June, what was your main work or activity?
- In July, what was your main work or activity?
- In August, what was your main work or activity?
- In September, what was your main work or activity?
- In October, what was your main work or activity?
- In November, what was your main work or activity?
- In December, what was your main work or activity?
- In January, what was your main work or activity?
- In February, what was your main work or activity?
- In March, what was your main work or activity?
- In April, what was your main work or activity?
- Can you tell me roughly what you earned in each month of the past year, or would it be easier to give one total for the whole year?
- Which range is closest to what you usually earn in a month from work? Under Rs 5,000; Rs 5,000 to 10,000; Rs 10,000 to 20,000; Rs 20,000 or more; or don't know?
- How much did you earn from all your work in May, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in June, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in July, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in August, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in September, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in October, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in November, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in December, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in January, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in February, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in March, after costs? (Rs)
- How much did you earn from all your work in April, after costs? (Rs)
- Thinking of the whole past year, about how much did you earn in total from all your work, after costs? (Rs)
- Out of every 100 rupees you earned in the whole year, how many came from your Yatra work? (The rest is counted as coming from your other work.)
- In the months when the Yatra is closed and you are doing other work, on a normal working day, how many hours do you work in total?
- In those months, how many days a week do you usually work?

## Chapter 6: Money sent and received

*Next, the money that goes home and the money that comes in from home.*

> अब उस पैसे के बारे में, जो घर भेजा जाता है और जो घर से आता है।

- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much money do you usually send to family or others living elsewhere? (Rs, 0 if none)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much do you usually send? (Rs, 0 if none)
- How do you usually send it?
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much money do you usually receive from family or others? (Rs, 0 if none)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much do you usually receive? (Rs, 0 if none)
- During the Yatra season, how many people are you feeding and spending on here, counting yourself?

## Chapter 7: What your household spends

*Now the everyday spending. Think of a normal month, and of the rest of the household if they live at home.*

> अब रोज़ के खर्च के बारे में। सामान्य महीने के बारे में सोचिए।

- Leaving aside money you send home — is what your household spends in a normal month during the Yatra season different from what it spends when the Yatra is closed?
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on cereals (rice, wheat, other grains), pulses, sugar and salt, bought or from your own stock? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on cereals (rice, wheat, other grains), pulses, sugar and salt, bought or from your own stock? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on milk and milk products, vegetables, fruit, egg/fish/meat, cooking oil, spices, and tea or coffee, bought or from your own stock? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on milk and milk products, vegetables, fruit, egg/fish/meat, cooking oil, spices, and tea or coffee, bought or from your own stock? (Rs)
- If you (and anyone staying with you here) had not grown, raised or been given any of your food, about how much would it have cost to buy in a normal month during the Yatra season? (Rs, 0 if you buy everything)
- If your household had not grown, raised or been given any of your food, about how much would it have cost to buy in a normal month when the Yatra is closed? (Rs, 0 if you buy everything)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on meals, tea and snacks eaten outside the home, including any an employer gave free, at their market value? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on meals, tea and snacks eaten outside the home, including any an employer gave free, at their market value? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on fuel and light: cooking fuel, firewood, electricity, kerosene, candles? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on fuel and light: cooking fuel, firewood, electricity, kerosene, candles? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on soap, toiletries, cleaning goods and other small household items? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on soap, toiletries, cleaning goods and other small household items? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on local transport (bus, shared jeep, auto) and phone or internet charges? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on local transport (bus, shared jeep, auto) and phone or internet charges? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on rent for the home (0 if owned, and 0 for any period spent living for free)? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on rent for the home (0 if owned, and 0 for any period spent living for free)? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on medicine, doctor's or clinic fees and tests, not counting a hospital stay? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on medicine, doctor's or clinic fees and tests, not counting a hospital stay? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on biscuits, namkeen, chips, packaged snacks, cold drinks or bottled water? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on biscuits, namkeen, chips, packaged snacks, cold drinks or bottled water? (Rs)
- In a normal month during the Yatra season, how much do you (and anyone staying with you here) spend on pan, gutka, bidi, cigarettes, tobacco or alcohol? (Rs)
- In a normal month when the Yatra is closed, how much does your household spend on pan, gutka, bidi, cigarettes, tobacco or alcohol? (Rs)
- In the last 12 months, how much did your household spend on clothes and footwear? (Rs)
- In the last 12 months, how much on education: fees, books, uniforms, tuition? (Rs)
- In the last 12 months, how much did your household pay for any hospital admission or stay? (Rs, 0 if none)
- In the last 12 months, how much on durable goods: furniture, utensils, cooking appliances, phone, jewellery or ornaments, bicycle or vehicle parts? (Rs)

- Does your usual home have an electricity connection?
- What kind of toilet does your household use at your usual home?
- What is your main source of drinking water at your usual home?
- Is the drinking water available at the house itself?
- How long does it take to go there, get the water and come back? (minutes, round trip)
- What does your household mainly use to cook at your usual home?
- Does your household own: a television?
- ... a radio?
- ... a bicycle?
- ... a motorcycle or scooter?
- ... a car, jeep or truck?
- ... a telephone of any kind, mobile or landline?
- ... a computer or laptop?

## Chapter 9: Money, loans and protection

*Now borrowing, insurance, government schemes and phones.*

> अब उधार, बीमा, सरकारी योजनाएँ और फ़ोन के बारे में।

- Does anyone in your household have a bank account or a post office account?
- In the last 12 months, did you or anyone in your household borrow money?
- Who was the main lender?
- What did you take it for?
- How much did you borrow in total? (Rs)
- How much of that loan is still left to repay? (Rs)
- Do you pay interest on it?
- On every 100 rupees you borrowed, about how much interest do you pay in a month? (Rs; 0 if none, leave blank if not known)
- Did you have to give something as security for it — an animal, a vehicle, jewellery, land or your business?
- What did you have to give as security?
- How many people in your household are covered by any health insurance or health scheme, including government ones? (0 if none)
- How many people in your household have life insurance? (0 if none)
- Is your crop insured?
- In the last 12 months, did your household get anything from any government scheme? Read the list out before you take an answer.
  - *Note for you:* READ THE LIST OUT; do not ask the bare question, because people fail to recall the category and say no. Ration card or PDS grain; MGNREGA work or job card; old-age, widow or disability pension; PM-KISAN; Ujjwala LPG; PM-SYM or Atal Pension; Ayushman Bharat or a health card; a housing scheme (PMAY or state). Any one of them is a Yes.
- Which ones? Write down every scheme they name, in their own words.
  - *Note for you:* Write the scheme names in their own words, all of them, including any not on the list you just read. Office-coded afterwards, so do not try to fit the answer to a category.
- Can you draw your ration here, or only at your home place?
- Do you own a smartphone?
- Do you ever use UPI or another way of paying by phone?
- How many people in your household can make a payment by phone themselves, without help? (0 if none)

## Chapter 10: Health

*Now health, for you and the people at home.*

> अब सेहत के बारे में, आपके और घर पर रहने वालों के लिए।

- In the last 15 days, was anyone in your household ill?
- How did the household mainly cope with the cost of this: used savings; borrowed money; sold or pawned assets; cut other consumption; got help from relatives or community; or something else?
- About how much did the household spend on this in the last 15 days (medicine, doctor or clinic fees, travel for care)? (Rs)
- Do you have difficulty walking or climbing steps?
- In the last 12 months, was anyone in your household admitted to hospital overnight?
- In the last 3 months, was there a time when someone in your household needed medical care but could not get it?
- In the last five years, has any child or young person under 18 in your household died?
- In the last five years, did any woman in your household give birth?
- For the most recent birth, did she have at least four check-ups before the delivery?
  - *Note for you:* Ask the mother herself if she is there. If not, probe: did she go for check-ups before the birth, and about how many times? Four or more is Yes; two or three is No. Do not accept a shrug — ask how many times she went.
- Who conducted that delivery — a doctor, a nurse or ANM, an ASHA or Anganwadi worker, a dai, or nobody trained?
  - *Note for you:* Code who CONDUCTED the delivery, not who was in the room. An ASHA or Anganwadi worker accompanying a birth is code 3, not a nurse: NITI counts only a doctor, nurse, ANM or LHV as skilled. A dai is code 4. If an ANM conducted it and an ASHA brought her, that is 2.

## Chapter 11: Hard times and getting by

*Everyone has bad times. Tell me about the hard times and how you got through them.*

> हर किसी के जीवन में मुश्किल समय आता है। बताइए कब आया और कैसे निकले।

- In the last 12 months, did any of these happen — to your household, or to the Yatra route you work on? Check every one that applies.
- Thinking of whichever of those hit you hardest — about how many weeks of work did you lose because of it? (0 if none)
- And about how much money did your household have to spend because of it — treatment, repairs, replacing what was lost? (Rs, 0 if nothing)
- And in which month did that one happen?
- How did your household manage? Check every one they used.
- In a normal week during the Yatra season, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household rely on less expensive/less preferred food? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week when the Yatra is closed, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household rely on less expensive/less preferred food? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week during the Yatra season, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household borrow food and/or rely on help from friends/relatives? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week when the Yatra is closed, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household borrow food and/or rely on help from friends/relatives? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week during the Yatra season, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household reduce the number of meals eaten per day? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week when the Yatra is closed, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household reduce the number of meals eaten per day? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week during the Yatra season, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household reduce portion size of meals? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week when the Yatra is closed, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household reduce portion size of meals? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week during the Yatra season, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household restrict consumption of adults/mothers in order for young children to eat? (0-7 days)
- In a normal week when the Yatra is closed, to cope with a lack of food or money to buy it, how many days did your household restrict consumption of adults/mothers in order for young children to eat? (0-7 days)

## Chapter 12: The conditions of your job

*Some questions about the conditions of the work you do.*

> अब आपके काम की स्थितियों के बारे में कुछ सवाल।

- Is your job: permanent; seasonal or temporary; occasional or casual; on probation; or fixed-term?
  - *Note for you:* Both will sound seasonal here — the Yatra closes for everyone. Ask about WITHIN the season. One employer keeps them on for a stretch = Seasonal or temporary. They take work day by day from whoever offers it = Occasional or casual. Self-employed: answer for how their own work runs. On probation and Fixed-term are rare here; do not reach for them.
- Do you have a signed contract? Yes, signed; yes but not yet signed; no contract.
- Is your workplace or business registered, for example with a taxpayer or GST number, a shop or trade licence, the Yatra registration, or a union?
- Do you contribute to any pension system? Yes, the employer deducts it; yes, voluntarily; no.
- Do you have health insurance through your work? Yes; only private or other insurance; none; don't know.
- Do you have the right to paid holiday, sick or maternity leave?
- Have you ever been physically injured at your workplace?
- In the last 12 months, was anyone physically injured at your workplace because of work?
- And in the last 12 months, did anyone die at your workplace because of work?
- Would you like to work more hours than you do?
- On a working day, how many MORE hours would you like to work?
- Across the months when you had no paid work, about how many MONTHS in total were you looking for work?
- Was your current work the first paid job you ever had?
- Have you completed any college, diploma or university course?
- Are you enrolled in any pension, insurance or social-security scheme? For example, EPFO or PM-SYM pension, or Ayushman Bharat health cover.
- For more than half of your working day, do you work at very high speed?
- For more than half of your working day, do you work to tight deadlines?
- For more than half of your working day, do you not have enough time to finish your tasks?
- For more than half of your working day, do you work in a tiring or painful position?
- For more than half of your working day, do you carry or move heavy loads?
- For more than half of your working day, do you make the same movements again and again?
- For more than half of your working day, are you exposed to loud noise?
- For more than half of your working day, are you exposed to extreme heat or cold?

## Chapter 14: Common land and forest

*Last, the common land, forest and water that people use around here.*

> आखिर में, इलाके की साझा ज़मीन, जंगल और पानी के बारे में।

- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, did you or anyone staying with you here collect firewood or dead wood from common land or forest in the Yatra region or workplace region?
- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, did you or anyone staying with you here cut grass or fodder from common land in the Yatra region or workplace region?
- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, did you or anyone staying with you here collect herbs, mushrooms or wild fruit from the forest in the Yatra region or workplace region?
- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, did any animal of yours or of anyone staying with you here graze on common pasture in the Yatra region or workplace region?
- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, has anyone you or someone staying with you here been stopped from using a common forest, pasture or path, or been asked to pay to use it, in the Yatra region or workplace region?
- In this Yatra season or last Yatra season, has a common place you used to collect from or graze on been closed, because of a landslide, flood, road or bridge damage, or construction work, in the Yatra region or workplace region?

## Chapter 15: The road ahead

- Do you think the ropeway will improve the livelihoods of people here? Yes; no; or don't know?
