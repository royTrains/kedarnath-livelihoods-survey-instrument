their indexed abstracts. **Abstract evidence is weaker than a methods section**, so each
row carries what the abstract actually supports:

- **CONFIRMED** — the abstract names the estimator (FGLS, three-stage, or Chaudhuri).
- **LIKELY** — the abstract names a vulnerability-to-poverty measure but not the estimator.
- **UNCLEAR** — neither appears; the FGLS phrase that found it sits in the full text.
- **NO ABSTRACT** — none indexed; cannot be screened this way.

Percentages are every figure the abstract reports, in order, as candidates for the
vulnerability-rate and poverty-rate columns. **They are not labelled by the index and must
be read against the paper before use.** Same for the threshold column.
